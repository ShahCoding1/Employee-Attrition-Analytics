# Employee Attrition Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

Employee turnover is an important workforce management challenge. This project analyzes employee data to explore patterns associated with employee attrition.

The project uses Python and statistical analysis techniques to examine demographic, professional, compensation, and workplace-related factors.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition
- Explore department and job-role differences
- Investigate overtime and attrition
- Analyze salary and age patterns
- Examine job satisfaction and work-life balance
- Study employee tenure and promotion patterns
- Apply statistical tests
- Generate business insights and recommendations

## 📊 Statistical Methods

### Chi-Square Test

Used to investigate associations between categorical variables and employee attrition.

### Independent Samples t-Test

Used to compare numerical variables between employees who stayed and employees who left.

### Correlation Analysis

Used to examine relationships between numerical variables and the binary attrition indicator.

## 💡 Business Value

The analysis can help HR teams identify workforce groups with different observed attrition patterns and determine areas that may require further investigation.

The findings are interpreted as associations rather than causal relationships.

## ⚠️ Limitations

The dataset represents a specific employee population and may not generalize to every organization.

The analysis does not establish causation and does not include all possible factors influencing employee turnover.
